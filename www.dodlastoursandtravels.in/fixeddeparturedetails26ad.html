GANGA SAGAR YATRA      

DAY 1      PURI AND KONARK      

MORNING REACHED BHUBANESWAR AFTER BREAK FAST PROCEED TO VISIT KONARK TEMPLE AFTER LUNCH VISIT PURI JAGANATH RETURN BHUBHANESWAR NIGHT HALT      


DAY 2      BHUBANESWAR AND LINGARAJ TEMPLE      

MORNING AFTER BREAK FAST VISIT TO LINGRAJ TEMPLE AND MUKTHESWAR AND AFTER LUNCH VISIT JAJIPUR      


DAY 2      JAIPUR GIRIJADEVI      

AFTER LUNCH VISIT JAJIPUR GIRIJADEVI SHAKTHIPET RETURN BHUBANESWAR RAILWAY STATION NIGHT BHUBANESWAR TO HOWHAR RTAIN JOURNY      


DAY 3      HOWRAH      

MORNING REACHED HOWRAH RAILWAY STATION AFTER BREAKFAST KOLKATHA LOCAL SIGHTEEING NIGHT HALT       


DAY 4      HOWRAH TO GUWAHATI TRAIN JOURNY      

HOWRAH TO GUWAHATI BY TRAIN JOURNY      


DAY 5       GUWAHATI LOCAL AND KAMAKYA      

MORNING VISIT KAMAKYA TEMPLE AND UMANAD SHUKLANADH AFTER LUNCH PROCEED TO BALAJI TEMPLE VIST VASISTA MUNI TEMPLE AND NAVA GRAHA NIGHT HALT GUWAHATI      


DAY 6      GUWAHATI TO RAMPURHAT TRAIN JOURNY      

MORNING AFTER BREAKFAST VIST MARKET AFTER LUNCH PROCEED RAILWAY STATION TRAIN JOURNY      


DAY 7       MAA TARA DEVI SHAKTHIPET DHARSHAN VASAKINADH      

MORNING REACHED RAMPUR HAT AFTER BREAKFAST VIST MAA TARA DEVI SHAKTHIPET AFTE LUNCH PROCEED TO MOS PWER FUL SHIVA TEMPLE VASAKINATH DHARSHAN NIGHT HALT VAIDHYANATH      


DAY 8      VAIDYANADH -DEOGHAR      

MORNING VISIT 5TH VAIDYANADH JYOTHILING DRSHAN AFTER LUNCH PROCEED TO MAYAPURI NIGHT HALT      


DAY 9       MAYAPURI AND NAWADEEP AND KALNA 108 SHIVA LINGA TEMPLE      

MORNING VISIT ISKHAN MAIN SOCITY VISIT MAYAPUR AND CHAITHANYA MAHA PRABU CITY       


DAY 9       KALNA 108 SHIVA LINGA TEMPLE      

AFTER LUNCH PROCEED TO KALNA TEMPLE AND SHUNKALA DEVI SAKTHI TEMPLE       


DAY 10      TARAKESWAR TEMPLE PANDUVA      

MORNING PROCEED TO PADUVA STUP AND VISIT TARAKESWAR TEMPLE AFTRH LUNCH PROCEED TO HOWRAH NIGHT HALT HOWRAH      


DAY 11       GANGA SAGAR YATRA      

HOWRAH TO GANGA SAGAR BY BOAT JOURNY. AFTER 5 HOURS JOURNY REACHED KACHBEDIYA THEN PROCEED TO GANGA SAGAR BY BUS AFTER ONE HOUR AFTER DINNER VISIT KAPILA MAHAMUNI ASHARM MIDNIGHT 3 CLOCK BATH GANGA SAGAR NIGHT HALT      


DAY 12       RETURN GANGA SAGR TO HOWRAH       

MORNING RETURN TO SHIP PICKUP POIN AFTER BREAKFAST PROCEED TO HOWRAH SHIP JOUARY NIGHT HALT HOWRAH       


DAY 13      Return Home with Wonderful Memories      

SHALIMAR TO VIJAYAWAD RETURN BY TRAIN REACHED NEST DAY AFTERNOON
