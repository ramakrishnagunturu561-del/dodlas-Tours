Airport

Piligrims travel to Shamshabad airpot from vijayawada on Friday


Kathmandu

Travelling from Hyderabad by Air lines we reach delhi, from there @3.00pm we will reach kathmandu and after having lunch pilgrims visit Panchamukeswara Temple(Night Stay)


Nepalgunj

Sunday Morning sight seeing in kathmandu and after lunch travelling on flight from kathmandu to Nepal-Ganjj (Night Halt) .


Simikot

By AirLines Piligrims travel from Nepal-Ganjj to Simikot,Simikot to Hilsa(by helicopter),Hilsa to Purang(by car 1 hour) And night halt either at hilsa or Purang


Purang

On Tuesday Sight Seeing of Purang and halting (to accustom to weather conditions)


Manasarovar

From Purang to Manasarovar(by car @3hrs) Piligrims reach Manasarovar and by there taking bath at holy lake and sight seeing of manasarovar lake


Manasarovar Lake

Piligrims perform holy rituals at ManasarovarLake by offering pooja and night halt @ manasarovar


Darchen

Its time to leave sarovar and pilgrims start from Dharchen to reach dehrapakk @evening(5000kms).Their will be a beautiful view of mountain Peaks which will be feast to eyes(night halt)


Parvathisthal

At Morning we start from dehrapark to dolmola kanuma,pilgrims will have a glance at Yamsthal,Gaurikund,Parvathisthal..with pleasing the senses we reach juthalpak @evening(night halt).


Hilsa

After finishing the enroute of Kalisa Mountain peaks we reach purang/hilsa @night(halt)


Enroute Katmandu

At morning we travel back to simikot from Hilsa,and Simikot to Nepalganjj enrouting kathmandu (night halt)


Delhi-return

Morning i.e on tuesday we reach delhi

by flight from kathmandu

and from delhi @ evening

we reach hyderabad/vijayawada.

Inclusions

  • Tibet Visa & Permit Fee [Normal].
  • Ground transfers in Tibet using Toyota Land Cruisers or bus as applicable.
  • Services of English speaking guide in Tibet.
  • Services of our trekking equipment and Sherpa staff.
  • All Trekking & camping equipment including Gyamo Bags, Sleeping bags and oxygen cylinders

Exclusions

  • using extra nights in lodge (except the mentioned on itinerary).
