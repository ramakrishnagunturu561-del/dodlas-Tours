North India Teertha & Jyotirlinga Circuit

Day-by-Day Sacred Schedule

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Paced specifically for family and senior citizen comfort with regular rest breaks and satvik meals.

Day 1

Vijayawada to Haridwar via Delhi

Depart from Vijayawada and reach Haridwar base camp with evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri.

Day 2

Rishikesh Holy Shrines & Mathura

Visit Triveni Ghat in Rishikesh, followed by travel to Lord Krishna's sacred birthplace Mathura and Vrindavan.

Day 3

Mathura, Vrindavan & Agra

Darshan at Krishna Janmabhoomi, Banke Bihari Temple, and visit historical Agra monument.

Day 4

Ayodhya Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan

Drive to Ayodhya. Holy dip in Sarayu River and grand darshan at Sri Ram Lalla Mandir and Kanak Bhavan.

Day 5

Prayagraj Triveni Sangam Snanam

Boat ride to sacred Triveni Sangam (confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswati) for holy snanam, Veni Daanam, and Bade Hanuman darshan.

Day 6

Prayagraj to Kashi (Varanasi)

Drive to Varanasi. Check into hotel and attend the sublime evening Ganga Aarti from private boats.

Day 7

Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga & Annapurna

Early morning sacred dip in River Ganga. Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple, Annapurna Devi, and Kalabhairava Temple.

Day 8

Kashi to Gaya (Vishnupada & Pindadaan)

Drive to Gaya. Perform sacred ancestral Pindadaan rituals at Sri Vishnupada Temple on the banks of Falgu River.

Day 9

Bodhgaya Mahabodhi Temple & Deoghar

Visit UNESCO Mahabodhi Temple and Bodhi Tree, then travel to Deoghar (Baidyanath Jyotirlinga).

Day 10

Baidyanath Jyotirlinga Darshan to Kolkata

Special morning darshan at Baba Baidyanath Jyotirlinga, then board coach for Kolkata.

Day 11

Kolkata Kali Temples & Ganga Sagar

Darshan at Kalighat and Dakshineswar Kali Temple. Excursion to sacred Ganga Sagar confluence.

Day 12

Puri Jagannath & Konark Sun Temple

Drive to Puri for evening darshan of Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra, and visit Konark Sun Temple.

Day 13

Bhubaneswar Lingaraj Temple & Return

Visit 11th century Lingaraj Temple in Bhubaneswar and return journey to Vijayawada.
