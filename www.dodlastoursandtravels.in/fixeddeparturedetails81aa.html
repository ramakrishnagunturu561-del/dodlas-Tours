Sacred Himalayan Circuit

8-Day Sacred Yatra Itinerary

Carefully paced for devotees and senior citizens with comfortable hotel halts, satvik dining, and scenic Himalayan vistas.

Day 01

Gorakhpur – Bhairahawa

Arrival at Gorakhpur Airport / Railway Station. On arrival, our representative will meet and take you to the vehicle and then proceed to visit Gorakhnath Temple. Afterwards proceed towards Bhairahawa. Upon arrival, check-in to hotel for overnight stay.

📍 Gorakhnath Temple 🏨 Bhairahawa Hotel 🚌 Border Crossing
Day 02

Bhairahawa – Pokhara & Visit Lumbini

Morning drive to Lumbini (25 Kms). Visit Maya Devi Temple (Birth Place of Lord Buddha — World Peace Pagoda and Thai Monastery) and back to hotel. After breakfast, proceed to Pokhara (200 Kms / 7-8 Hrs). Pokhara is the most popular destination in Nepal after Kathmandu. Enjoy the scenic mountain drive and check in to hotel.

📍 Lumbini Maya Devi Temple ⛰ Scenic Mountain Route 🏨 Pokhara Leisure
Day 03

Pokhara – Muktinath – Jomsom

Post breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Muktinath (180 Kms / 5 Hrs), visit holy Muktinath Temple. We walk to the temple for 25 minutes or take local jeeps/horses. Perform sacred bath in 108 holy water spouts (Mukti Dhara) and darshan of Lord Sri Muktinath, followed by Jwala Mai Temple. Transfer to Jomsom for overnight stay.

📍 Sri Muktinath Dham 💧 108 Sacred Water Spouts ⛰ Jomsom Altitude Stay
Day 04

Jomsom – Pokhara Sightseeing

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive towards Pokhara. On arrival, proceed with visits to Bindhyabasini Temple, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave, Davis Falls (Patale Chhango), and holy Barahi Temple situated on an island in scenic Phewa Lake with boat ride. Overnight stay at Pokhara.

⛵ Phewa Lake Boating 📍 Barahi Island Temple 📍 Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave
Day 05

Pokhara – Manakamana Temple – Kathmandu

Morning after breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive to Kathmandu (210 Kms / 8-10 Hrs). Enroute visit wish-fulfilling Manakamana Temple by aerial Cable Car ride over the Trishuli river valley. On arrival in Kathmandu, check-in to hotel for overnight stay.

🚔 Aerial Cable Car 📍 Manakamana Devi Darshan 🏨 Kathmandu Capital Arrival
Day 06

Full Day Sightseeing of Kathmandu

After breakfast, proceed to visit the sacred Pashupatinath Temple on the banks of Bagmati river, Guheshwori Temple (Maha Shakti Peeth), Boudhanath Stupa, and Kathmandu Durbar Square. Evening at leisure for shopping spiritual rudraksha and souvenirs. Overnight stay at Kathmandu.

📍 Sri Pashupatinath Temple 📍 Guheshwori Shakti Peeth 📍 Boudhanath Stupa
Day 07

Kathmandu – Janakpur Dham

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Janakpur (200 Kms / 5-6 Hrs), also known as Janakpur Dham, the holy birthplace of Goddess Sita. Visit the magnificent Janaki Mandir, Ram Janaki Vivah Mandap, and sacred holy ponds. Overnight stay at Janakpur.

📍 Historic Janaki Mandir 📍 Ram Janaki Vivah Mandap 🏨 Janakpur Overnight
Day 08

Janakpur – Darbhanga (Departure Drop)

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Darbhanga (85 Kms / 2 Hrs) via Madhubani, famous for its Mithila paintings. On arrival, transfer to Darbhanga Airport / Railway Station for return journey home with divine blessings and sacred memories.

✈ Darbhanga Airport Drop 🎨 Madhubani Heritage 🙏 Divine Blessings

Important Yatra Guidelines

Preparation & Travel Requirements

1

Valid Identification for Nepal Entry

Indian citizens require either an original valid Indian Passport (with minimum 6 months validity) or an original Election Voter ID card issued by Election Commission of India. Aadhaar card or driving licenses are not accepted as border travel documents.

2

Weather & High Altitude Clothing

Muktinath is located at an altitude of approximately 3,710 metres (12,172 ft). Yatris are advised to carry heavy woollen thermals, jackets, woollen caps, gloves, and comfortable walking shoes. Jomsom experiences strong winds in the afternoon.

3

Senior Citizen Care & Special Assistance

Our experienced tour managers provide personalized care for elderly parents. Jeeps, horses, and local porters can be arranged at Muktinath for devotees who prefer not to walk the 25-minute ascent.

4

Currency & Payments

Indian currency notes of denomination ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, and ₹10 are widely accepted across Nepal. Denominations of ₹500 notes are subject to periodic regulatory restrictions.
