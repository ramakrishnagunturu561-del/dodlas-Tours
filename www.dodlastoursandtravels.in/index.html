The Dodla Legacy

Pioneering Pilgrimage with Purity & Reverence Since 1962

Founded in Vijayawada by the venerated Yatra Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao, Dodla's Tours & Travels was created to solve a profound dilemma: allowing devotees to undertake demanding holy circuits without anxiety or logistical hardship.

“For 64 unbroken years, our tours have been conducted with divine devotion by Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao, and faithfully elevated with modern comfort by his elder son Dodla Hemanth Kumar.”
DODLA HEMANTH KUMAR • MANAGING DIRECTOR

Generations of families across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu travel exclusively with Dodla's because we treat every traveler not as a customer, but as an honoured pilgrim entrusted to our stewardship.

Pure Satvik Kitchen

Sanctuary Stays

Unhurried Senior Care
