Privacy Policy

Dodla's Tours Privacy Commitment

Dodla's Tours & Travels respects your privacy and recognizes the need to protect the personally identifiable information you share with us (such as name, address, phone number, and ID details for yatra permits). We follow rigorous standards when it comes to safeguarding your personal data.

Information Collection & Use

When you request a bespoke quote or book a sacred pilgrimage, we collect necessary traveler details (including full names, age, contact telephone numbers, and government identification numbers required by temple shrine boards, helicopter operators, and hotel partners).

Confidentiality & Security

Your personal details are never sold, leased, or traded to third-party marketing companies. Data is exclusively utilized to secure darshan passes, helicopter tickets, hotel vouchers, and transport logistics.
