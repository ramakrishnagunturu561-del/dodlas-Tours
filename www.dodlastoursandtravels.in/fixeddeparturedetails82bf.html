KERALA TOUR      

      Day 1 Arrive Cochin      

Arrive Cochin. Transfer to the hotel. Afternoon visit Jewish Synagogue, Dutch Palace, Santa Cruz Basilica and Chinese fishing nets. Evening enjoy the boat ride by Sagar Rani (on direct payment). Night Stay COCHIN      


      Day 2 Cochin –Munnar      

After breakfast, proceed to Munnar ( 130 kms. / 4 hrs.), one of the most popular hill stations in Southern India known for its sprawling tea gardens. Evening at leisure. Night Stay MUNNAR      


      Day 3 Munnar – Sightseeing      

After breakfast, we drive through the tea estates and visit Mettupatty Dam to enjoy a boat ride, Echo point, Kundala lake. VISIT NILGIRI TAHR Night Stay Munnar      


      DAY 4 Munnar –Thekkady      

After breakfast, proceed to Thekkady ( 120 kms. /3.5 hrs.). Set in the heart of the Cardamom Hills is the Periyar National Park. Enjoy a boat cruise (on direct payment) on Periyar lake to view the varied wildlife of this region. Night Stay Thekkady      


      Day 5 Thekkady LOCAL SIGHT SEEING      

EVENING VISIT KALARI AND KADHAKALI DANCE NIGHT HALT TEKKAADY      


      DAY 6 Thekkady –Alleppey      

e To Alleppey ( 125 Kms. / 3.5 Hrs.) And Check Into Hotel. Later Enjoy The Famous Back Waters Ride. Night Stay Alleppey      


      DAY 7 Alleppey –Kovalam/Trivandrum      

Post Breakfast, Proceed To Trivandrum (135 Kms. / 3 Hrs.), The Capital City Of Kerala .Upon Arrival At Trivandrum, Check Into Hotel. Later Drive To Kovalam (18 Kms). Have Fun At Kovalam Beach And Back To Hotel. Night Stay Kovalam/Trivandrum      


      DAY 8 Kovalam /Trivandrum – Kanyakuamri      

Morning drive to Kanyakumari (79 kms. /1.5 hrs. ), check into hotel. Later visit Trisamudra Milan, Gandhi Mandapam, Kanyakumari Mandir. Take a ferry boat to Swami Vivekananda Rock Memorial. Night Stay Kanyakuamri      


      DAY 9 Kanyakumari – Trivandrum       

Early morning enjoy sunrise at Kanyakumari. Proceed to Trivandrum ( 80 kms./ 2 hrs.). Visit Shri Anantha Padmanabhaswami temple. Evening Transfer to Airport / Railway Station for your onward journey
