Day-Wise Sacred Itinerary

Interactive step-by-step spiritual journey timeline
DAY 1 OF 7
DAY 1

Arrive Cochin – Fort Kochi Heritage

Arrival at Cochin Airport / Railway Station. Check-in to hotel. Visit Fort Kochi, iconic Chinese Fishing Nets, and Mattancherry Dutch Palace.

Cochin Arrival
DAY 2

Cochin to Munnar Tea Country (130 Kms)

Ascend through misty Western Ghats. Enroute stops at Cheeyappara and Valara Waterfalls and lush spice plantations. Arrive Munnar.

Cheeyappara Waterfalls
DAY 3

Munnar Sightseeing – Eravikulam & Mattupetty

Visit Eravikulam National Park (home of Nilgiri Tahr), Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, and the Tata Tea Museum.

Eravikulam National Park
DAY 4

Munnar to Thekkady (Periyar Wildlife)

Drive through cardamom hills to Thekkady. Boat safari on Periyar Lake observing wild elephants and birds, and attend Kathakali dance.

Periyar Lake Boating
DAY 5

Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat Cruise

Board a traditional deluxe Kerala Kettuvallam (Houseboat). Cruise through serene palm-fringed backwaters, paddy fields, and lagoons.

Deluxe Houseboat
DAY 6

Alleppey to Guruvayur Sri Krishna Temple

Drive to Guruvayur. Holy evening darshan of Lord Guruvayurappan (Bhookaikuntha), attend deeparadhana, and visit Mammiyoor Shiva Temple.

Guruvayurappan Darshan
DAY 7

Guruvayur to Cochin Drop

Morning darshan at Punnathur Kotta elephant sanctuary, followed by transfer to Cochin Airport / Railway Station for return to Vijayawada.

Elephant Sanctuary
Journey Complete • Divine Blessings

Important Travel Guidelines & Devotee Care

Essential preparation tips for a comfortable and meaningful spiritual journey
Dress Code:

Traditional modest attire is required at all sacred shrines. For men: Dhoti / Kurta. For women: Sarees or modest Salwar suits.

Senior Citizen Health & Priority Care:

Please carry sufficient personal prescribed medications. Our tour managers give special priority to elderly pilgrims at boarding and lodging points.

Satvik Dining Guarantee:

Pure vegetarian satvik food cooked fresh following traditional customs, respecting senior dietary preferences.

Identity Documentation:

Carry original government photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) along with copies for hotel check-ins and security checkpoints.
