Excellence in Motion

Everything Taken Care Of With Devotion

From hot Satvik meals in the high Himalayas to private darshan queues in South India, our end-to-end services ensure your yatra is sacred and joyous.

Sacred Pilgrimage Escort & Stewardship

64 years of specialized expertise in coordinating Himalayan and national teertha yatras. Experienced managers accompany every tour, organizing temple entries, pujas, and sankalpams.

  • Telugu & English speaking senior managers
  • VIP Darshan & Archana assistance
  • Traditional ritual coordination
  • Luggage & porter coordination

100% Pure Satvik South Indian Dining

On all our extended yatras, a dedicated culinary team of traditional South Indian cooks travels alongside, serving piping hot breakfast, lunch, and dinner with pure filtered water.

  • Traditional South Indian satvik recipes
  • Strictly no onion, no garlic options
  • Freshly cooked hot meals thrice daily
  • Pure packaged drinking water

Senior Citizen Paced & Dedicated Care

We understand the physical challenges faced by our respected elders. Every itinerary is planned with generous rest intervals, zero night-travel exhaustion, and caring attention.

  • Ground floor hotel rooms where available
  • Pony, doli, and battery car booking help
  • First-aid medical kits on all buses
  • Attentive staff helping boarding & deboarding

Own Luxury Bus Fleet & Coach Transport

Dodla's Tours operates its own modern transport infrastructure: 2x2 AC Pushback Coaches, Volvo Multi-Axle buses, Force Travellers, and Innova Crystas driven by mountain-tested chauffeurs.

  • Air suspension pushback seats
  • Individual mobile charging points
  • Hi-definition audio/video entertainment
  • Regular mechanical safety inspections

Train & Flight Group Ticketing Desks

Seamless connectivity for yatris joining from across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and beyond. We organize group train reservations, Tatkal bookings, and flight connections.

  • Group train bookings in 3AC / Sleeper
  • Domestic & International flight booking
  • Airport & railway station pickups
  • Connection management

Customized Family & Corporate Tours

Looking for a private family holiday or customized temple circuit? Our Vijayawada planners craft bespoke itineraries matching your chosen dates, hotel category, and vehicle preference.

  • Tailor-made itineraries for small families
  • Choice of 3-star, 4-star, or heritage hotels
  • Dedicated chauffeur-driven private car
  • Flexible stopovers and darshan timings
