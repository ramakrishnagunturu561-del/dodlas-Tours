Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sacred yatras, satvik dining, senior citizen care, booking, and bus transportation.

Home / FAQ

1. How is food arranged during the sacred yatras?

On all our major tours (including Chardham, Amarnath, South India, and North India), a dedicated team of traditional South Indian cooks travels with the group. We prepare 100% pure vegetarian satvik meals (hot breakfast, lunch, and dinner) using purified drinking water. We do not depend on roadside commercial eateries.

2. Can elderly parents travel alone on these yatras?

Yes, absolutely. Founded by Yatra Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao with elderly devotees at heart, our senior tour managers provide personal attention, help with luggage, coordinate ground-floor hotel rooms, and assist with booking ponies, dolis, or battery cars for hill climbs.

3. What type of buses and vehicles are provided?

We operate our own modern 2x2 AC Pushback Coaches equipped with air suspension, comfortable reclining seats, charging ports, and audio/video facilities. For smaller family groups, we provide 12/17-seater Tempo Travellers and Innova Crystas driven by mountain-experienced chauffeurs.

4. How do we book a tour and confirm our seats?

You can book by visiting our head office in Vijayawada (Kaleswara Rao Market, Besant Road) or by contacting our primary calling desk at +91 98481 24759 / +91 94945 31001. We will share departure dates, seat charts, and payment instructions on WhatsApp.

5. What documents are required for Amarnath and Chardham?

Pilgrims need a valid government photo ID (Aadhaar Card or Voter ID) and passport-size photographs. For Amarnath and Kailash yatras, a Compulsory Health Certificate (CHC) signed by an authorized medical practitioner is mandatory. We provide complete guidance for securing these permits.
