SINGAPORE, MALESYA, WITH CRUISE      

      DAY 1 : HYD/MAA/BLR – SINGAPORE      

Onto sensational Singapore – theLion City! Enjoy the sights and sounds of Singapore. On arrival Singapore, you shall clear your visa and immigration process later proceed to check in hotel. Later in the evening you shall be taken togardens by the bay. Tonight enjoy a delicious dinner at a local Indian restaurant. Overnightstay at hotel in Singapore. (Lunch & Dinner)      


      DAY 2 : SINGAPORE CITY TOUR - SENTOSA ISLAND TOUR      

Enjoy the sights and sounds of Singapore on your city tour – Enjoy an exciting visit Sentosa Island – Singapore’s premier island resort getaway. After the continental breakfast you shall be taken to Singapore city tour an exciting tour of this colonial city. Drive past the Parliament House, Supreme Court, City Hall, China Town and Little India. Stop at Merlion, one of the most historical sites. Visit Thian Hock Keng Temple (Temple of Heavenly Happiness), the oldest and most important temple in Singapore. Post lunch you shall be driven to Sentosa Island to enjoy a fun-filled evening. Visit the S.E.A Oceanarium where you can venture into the ocean depths. Later prepare to be spellbound by ‘Wings of time’ - a show of characters, sound, light and laser extravaganza. Tonight enjoy a delicious dinner at a local Indian restaurant. Overnightstay at hotel in Singapore.( Breakfast, lunch &Dinner)      


      DAY 3 UNIVERSAL STUDIOS - SINGAPORE      

Onto sensational Singapore – theLion City! Enjoy the sights and sounds of Singapore. Today after continental breakfastyoushall be taken to universalstudio. Whole day atuniversal,enjoy your fun filled day with Asia’stopnotch Shows and rides. After Indian dinner overnightstay at hotel. (Breakfast,meal coupon at universal &Dinner)      


      DAY 4 SINGAPORE -JOHOR BAHRU      

Enjoy the sights and sounds of Johor bahru. After the continental breakfast sit back in the comforts of your coach, as we drive to Johor bahru which is approximately 40 kms from Singapore. After check in later evening you shall be taken to Johor Bahru city tour, afterthe Indian dinner overnightstay at Johor bahru. (Breakfast,lunch &Dinner) Onto Kuala Lumpur.      


      DAY 5 JOHOR BAHRU - KUALA LUMPUR      

Enjoy the sights and soundsof Kuala Lumpuron your exciting city tour. After the continental breakfast at your hotel Proceed to Kualalumpur which is approximately 330 kms from Johor bahru. Capital state of Malaysia Once reached youshall be taken to Putrajaya tour (Administrative captial) check into your hotel later proceed to kualalumpur city night tour. After the indian dinner Overnight stay in hotel kulalaumpur. (Breakfast,Lunch & Dinner      


      DAY 6 KUALALUMPUR – GENTING HIGHLANDS      

Onto Genting highlands.Enjoy the sights and sounds of Genting Highlands on yourtour. After the continental breakfast sit back in the comforts of your coach, as we drive to the mysterious Genting highlands which is approximately 1 hour 30 minutes journey. Enroute Batu Caves. Later drive to the sky way cable car station for a relaxing, scenic ride up to Genting highlands. Enjoy the rest of the day in genting at Skyworld outdoor theme park. You can try your luck at the largest and only land based casino in Asia-casinoDe-Genting (on your own). Overnight at Genting Highlands. (Breakfast, lunch&Dinner)      


      DAY 7 KUALALUMPUR– BANGKOK- PATTAYA      

Welcome to Bangkok – The City of Angels!Onto Pattaya – Asia’s premier beach resort. After continental breakfast you shall take your flight to bangkok. Upon arrival in Bangkok, we shall Complete visa on arrival process, preceded by immigration. You will be greeted by your friendly local representative at the airport. Sit back in the comforts of your coach, as we drive to the beautiful beach resort of Pattaya which lies 150-kms southeast of Bangkok. Upon arrival at Pattaya, We shall check in to the hotel. Later evening you shall be taken to Alcazar show a world-famous cabaret that can give the Lido and Paris’ Moulin Rouge, a run for their money. Featuring a grand theatre with 1,200 seats, mega watts of pulsating sound, a state-of-the-art light & sound system and ultra-modern computer technology, the Alcazar is a unique blend of acting, stage design, costumes and glamorous actors. Overnight at the hotel.      


      DAY 8 PATTAYA CORAL ISLANDTOUR– PANORAMICCITY TOUROF PATTAYA      

Visit Coral Island – an idyllic island of tranquility for coral viewing and water sports activities. Visit the Nong Nooch village, a traditional Thai village. This morning after continental breakfast at the hotel get set to explore the charm and beauty of Coral Island (subject to weather conditions). Start with an exciting ride in a speedboat. Here you can enjoy various water sports activities like parasailing, snorkeling, windsurfing, sea walking etc. on your own (Do not forget to carry your swimwear). Today enjoy Indian veg & non veg lunch at a local restaurant or at the Coral Island. Later in the afternoon, visit the famous Noong Nooch village, a traditional Thai village with beautiful tropical gardens. Witness an exciting Thai cultural show of lights, sounds and see the world famous elephants perform stunts. After the Indian dinner overnight at Hotel. (Breakfast, lunch&Dinner)      


      DAY 9 PATTAYA –BANGKOK – GOLDENBUDDHA &MARBLEBUDHA TEMPLE TOUR      

Onto Bangkok, En-Route Visits The Biggest Gem’s Gallery In Asia. Enjoy The Sights And Sounds Of Bangkok On Your Guided Temple City Tour. After An American Breakfast At The Hotel,Sit Back And Relax Inyour Coach, As We Drive You To Bangkok - The City Of Angels. Enroute Tiger Park And Visit The Gems Gallery Where You Not Only Have An Opportunity To Buy Precious Stones, But Also Get To Understand Their Creation And Making On A Fun Filled Tram Ride. Later Proceed On An Exciting Guided Tour Of The Bustling City Of Bangkok Considered To Be “The Perfect Architecture Of Thai Art”. Visit Marble Temple.Which Is The Oldest Temple In Bangkok. Also Visit ‘Wat Traimit’ The Temple Of The Golden Buddha. Enjoy An Indian Lunch Specially Arranged For You. After That Free For Shopping.Tonight, Enjoy A Delicious Dinner At A Local Indian Restaurant Overnightstay At Hotel.       DAY 10 FULLY DAY SAFARIWORLD &MARINE PARK TOUR      

Enjoy a fun-filled day at Safari World and Marine Park, Thailand’s greatest open-air Zoo and Leisure Park. Today enjoy an American Breakfast at the hotel and later prepare for some adventure and entertainment in Safari World and Marine Park – Thailand’s greatest open air zoo and Leisure Park. A drive through the scenic Safari Park presents a unique opportunity forthe closestencounter possible with rare andendangered species of the animal kingdom. Today enjoy a delicious lunch at Safari world. Whether it is an exciting safari drive through a picturesque African wilderness setting in the open zoo, an intimate encounter with the friendly dolphins and playful sea lions or an exhilarating fast-paced action stunt, spectacular Safari World has it all. Evening we are free for shopping. After the Indian dinner overnightstay at hotel. (Breakfast, lunch&Dinner).

We are confident You will join us again, sometime soon..(Breakfast either in the hotel or on board the flight).
