South India Maha Temple Circuit

15 days exploring Rameswaram, Madurai Meenakshi, Thanjavur Brihadeeswara, Srirangam, Kanyakumari, and Trivandrum with pure Satvik food.

Day-by-Day Sacred Schedule

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Paced specifically for family and senior citizen comfort with regular rest breaks and satvik meals.

Day 1

Vijayawada to Kanchipuram

Depart from Vijayawada in 2x2 AC Pushback coach. Reach holy city of Kanchipuram and visit Kamakshi Amman and Ekambareswarar temples.

Day 2

Kanchipuram to Mahabalipuram & Chidambaram

Visit UNESCO Shore Temples of Mahabalipuram, then drive to Chidambaram Nataraja Temple for evening Akasha Linga darshan.

Day 3

Kumbakonam & Thanjavur Brihadeeswara

Visit Kumbeswarar and Sarangapani temples in Kumbakonam, then marvel at the 1000-year UNESCO Great Living Chola Temple at Thanjavur.

Day 4

Thanjavur to Srirangam (1st Divya Desam)

Grand morning darshan at Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple in Srirangam, the foremost of the 108 Vaishnava Divya Desams, and Tiruvanaikaval water temple.

Day 5

Srirangam to Madurai Meenakshi Temple

Drive to temple city of Madurai. Evening darshan and archana at Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple.

Day 6

Madurai to Holy Rameswaram Island

Cross the scenic Pamban bridge. Hotel check-in and evening darshan at Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple.

Day 7

Rameswaram 22 Teerthams & Dhanushkodi

Perform sacred bath in Agni Teertham ocean and all 22 inside temple wells. Excursion to Rama Sethu viewpoint at Dhanushkodi.

Day 8

Rameswaram to Kanyakumari (Triveni Sangam)

Drive along the southern coast to Kanyakumari. Visit Bhagavathi Amman Temple and Vivekananda Rock Memorial.

Day 9

Kanyakumari Sunrise & Trivandrum Padmanabhaswamy

Witness glorious three-sea sunrise, then drive to Trivandrum for darshan of Lord Anantha Padmanabhaswamy in Ananthasayanam posture.

Day 10

Trivandrum to Palani Murugan Temple

Drive to sacred hill shrine of Sri Dhandayuthapani Swamy (Palani) and participate in evening golden chariot darshan.

Day 11

Palani to Sripuram Golden Temple (Vellore)

Drive to Vellore. Visit magnificent Sri Lakshmi Narayani Golden Temple adorned in 1,500 kg of pure gold.

Day 12

Vellore to Tirupati / Vijayawada Return

Return journey to Vijayawada with hearts filled with peace, divine blessings, and lifelong devotion.
