Our Sacred Heritage

The story of Yatra Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao and six decades of devotional service to pilgrims across India.

The Origins

Consecrated in 1962 with Pure Devotion

In 1962, Yatra Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao set out on a sacred mission from Vijayawada: to create an unblemished pilgrimage institution where elderly devotees and families could perform the most demanding holy yatras without anxiety, hardship, or logistical obstacles.

“From past 64 years without any failures, the sacred tours were conducted with divine devotion by Tapasvi Dodla Ramarao, and faithfully maintained with modern comfort under the personal stewardship of his elder son Dodla Hemanth Kumar.”
DODLA HEMANTH KUMAR • MANAGING DIRECTOR

Over the decades, tens of thousands of pilgrims from across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and North India have joined our sacred journeys. Their heartfelt blessings and unwavering trust are our greatest treasure.

Today, Dodla's Tours & Travels continues this sacred tradition — combining the devotion of a pilgrimage master with modern luxury transport, verified star accommodations, and hygienic South Indian satvik cuisine.

Pure Satvik Kitchen

Dedicated South Indian traditional cooks accompany every major tour, preparing fresh, hot, hygienic vegetarian meals seasoned with devotion.

Sanctuary Accommodations

Personally inspected, serene hotels and dharamshalas situated within walking distance or short transit to temple praharams.

Compassionate Senior Care

Paced itineraries, step-free access assistance, medical accessibility, and personal attendants ensuring elders travel with complete serenity.

Deluxe Luxury Coaches

High-roof 2x2 luxury AC pushback coaches handled by elite mountain captains with flawless safety track records.
