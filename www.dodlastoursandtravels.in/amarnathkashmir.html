Amarnath & Kashmir Shivalinga Yatra

16 days covering Holy Amarnath Cave, Katra Vaishno Devi, Golden Temple Amritsar, Dal Lake Shikara, and 5 Sacred Shakti Peethams with Satvik food.

Home / Pilgrimages / Amarnath & Kashmir Shivalinga Yatra
Day-by-Day Sacred Schedule

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Paced specifically for family and senior citizen comfort with regular rest breaks and satvik meals.

Day 1

Delhi Arrival & Departure to Amritsar

Group gathers in Delhi and boards AC coach for Amritsar with dinner stopover.

Day 2

Amritsar Golden Temple & Wagah Border

Morning darshan at Sri Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple) and Jallianwala Bagh. Evening visit to Wagah Border retreat ceremony.

Day 3

Amritsar to Katra (Maa Vaishno Devi Base)

Drive through scenic Punjab and Jammu plains to Katra. Check into hotel and prepare for Vaishno Devi yatra.

Day 4

Maa Vaishno Devi Bhavan Darshan

Ascend to holy Vaishno Devi Bhavan via pony, battery car, or trek. Holy darshan of Mahakali, Mahalakshmi, and Mahasaraswati pindis.

Day 5

Katra to Srinagar via Jawahar Tunnel

Scenic mountain drive through Jawahar Tunnel and Banihal Pass entering the breathtaking Kashmir valley. Check in to Srinagar.

Day 6

Srinagar Dal Lake Shikara & Shankaracharya Math

Morning Shikara boat ride on peaceful Dal Lake. Climb to Adi Shankaracharya Temple atop Gopadari Hill and visit Mughal Gardens.

Day 7

Srinagar to Baltal / Sonmarg Base Camp

Drive through Sindh river valley via Sonmarg to Baltal pilgrim base camp. Medical registration and briefing for holy cave.

Day 8

Holy Amarnath Cave Darshan (Ice Shivalinga)

Trek, pony, or helicopter to the Holy Cave of Amarnath. Divine darshan of the Swayambhu Natural Ice Shivalinga and return to Baltal.

Day 9

Baltal to Gulmarg Snow Meadows

Drive to scenic Gulmarg. Ride the world-famous Gulmarg Gondola cable car up to snow-clad Apharwat Peak.

Day 10

Gulmarg to Jammu Raghunath Temple

Drive back through the valley to Jammu city. Visit the historic Raghunath Temple complex.

Day 11

Jammu to Kangra (Jwalamukhi Shakthi Peetham)

Enter Himachal Pradesh. Darshan at the miraculous eternally burning flame at Jwalamukhi Shakthi Peetham.

Day 12

Chamunda Devi & Kangra Vajreshwari Temple

Visit Sri Chamunda Nandikeswar Dham and Nagarkot Kangra Vajreshwari Devi Temple.

Day 13

Chintpurni Devi & Naina Devi Shakthi Peethams

Holy darshan at Maa Chintpurni Devi Temple and Naina Devi Temple overlooking Gobind Sagar lake.

Day 14

Bhakra Nangal Dam & Chandigarh Rock Garden

Visit the engineering marvel Bhakra Nangal Dam and Nek Chand's unique Rock Garden in Chandigarh.

Day 15

Chandigarh to Delhi Local Sightseeing

Drive to Delhi. Visit Birla Mandir and India Gate with farewell satvik dinner.

Day 16

Delhi Market & Departure for Home

Free time for shopping in Delhi markets and boarding return train/flight to Vijayawada.
